Privacy

This statement explains what this personal portfolio collects, how website analytics are used, and the choices available to you.

Information This Site Collects

This site does not have user accounts, a contact form, or an on-site payment system. It uses Google Analytics to collect website usage information as described below. If you choose to contact me through an email or external profile link, the information you send is handled through that service rather than submitted to this website.

Google Analytics and Website Usage

This site uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand general website use. The analytics script is loaded after the page finishes loading. GA4 may collect information such as pages viewed, approximate location, browser and device type, referring source, visit timing, and interactions with selected links or features.

The site also sends a limited set of interaction events, such as resume downloads, LinkedIn or GitHub link clicks, and use of the About page’s fun-fact button. Those custom events do not include names, email addresses, message contents, or other information entered by a visitor.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Google Analytics may use cookies or similar browser technologies to distinguish visits and measure how the site is used. Analytics storage is enabled by default in the site’s current configuration, while advertising storage and ad-personalization signals are disabled.

Contact Links

This portfolio provides email, LinkedIn, GitHub, and phone contact details rather than an on-site form. If you follow one of those links or contact me directly, you choose what information to provide. Your email, phone, browser, or external platform may process that communication under its own privacy terms.

How Information Is Used

Website usage information is used to understand which pages and portfolio features are useful and to improve the site’s content and experience. Information you voluntarily send through a contact method is used to read and respond to your message.

This portfolio does not use advertising features and does not sell visitor information.

Third-Party Services and External Links

Google Analytics provides website measurement, and Google Fonts supplies the site’s typefaces from Google’s servers. The portfolio also links to external sites and services, including LinkedIn, GitHub, and project websites. When you load or visit a third-party service, that provider may process information according to its own privacy practices. This statement does not cover external websites.

Your Privacy Choices

You can limit or delete cookies through your browser settings, use private-browsing or tracking-protection features, or use a content blocker. You can also install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Blocking analytics does not prevent you from viewing the portfolio or using its contact links.

Privacy Questions

For questions about this statement or the portfolio’s privacy practices, email aletia.trepte@gmail.com.
